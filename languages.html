Language overview

Explore Sierra Leonean languages

This page introduces the featured languages and connects them to the searchable word examples on the homepage.

Krio

Krio is an English-based creole and a widely used language in Sierra Leone. It connects communities through shared everyday expressions.

Limba

Limba is one of the oldest indigenous languages of Sierra Leone and is primarily associated with the northern region.

Temne

Temne is a major language widely spoken across the Northern Province and the Western Area.
